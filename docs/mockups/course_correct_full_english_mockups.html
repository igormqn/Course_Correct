CHEM 421: Forensic Chemistry – Assignment 3

Tariq St. Patrick · Submitted 01/12/2025 · ⭐ Premium

Toxicological Analysis

Gas chromatography is an analytical technique allowing the separation and identification of volatile compounds in a biological sample.


In this study, we analyzed three blood samples collected from subjects exposed to an unknown toxic agent.


Method used: GC-MS (gas chromatography–mass spectrometry)


Results indicate the presence of aliphatic alcohols at abnormal concentrations (0.8 g/L), suggesting acute ethyl intoxication associated with the presence of metabolites suggesting secondary exposure to organophosphate compounds...


Conclusion: Based on the chromatographic profiles obtained, we can conclude that the primary toxicological agent is ethanol combined with trace amounts of acetaldehyde metabolites.
